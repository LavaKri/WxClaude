# Contributing

WxClaude is developed by one person and does not accept contributions: pull requests are not
accepted, and the ones that are opened will be closed without a review.

You can still read the code, copy it into your own repository (fork) and work there, within what the
license of the project allows (see `README.md`).

If you find a defect, you may describe it in an issue when issues are enabled on the repository.
Say what you did, what you expected and what happened, the version of WxClaude (*Help > About*) and
the screen reader you use. There is no promise of an answer or of a fix.
